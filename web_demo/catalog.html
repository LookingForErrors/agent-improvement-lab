About this collection

Thirty working scenario families from the existing English routing corpus, each with six scripted outcomes

Curation
Short requests, frustration, corrections, topic changes and longer ticket context. First-person wording was repaired in a separate version
Intent
Destinations and failure variants are authored scenario intent. They are not independent judge assessments or human gold
Context
The five long histories are synthetic stress fixtures with repeated archived records
Limits
No model execution, measured improvement or failure-rate estimate. Held-out raw scenarios and real annotations are excluded
